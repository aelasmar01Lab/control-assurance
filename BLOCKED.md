# Blocked frameworks

Frameworks the agent could not source under its rules: no identifier,
title or text is written from memory, and no third-party copy of a
standard is used as the source. Each entry says what was tried and what
is needed. Nothing here has a pack.

## SOC 2 (AICPA Trust Services Criteria)

**Tier:** identifiers only. The criteria are copyright AICPA.

**What was tried, 2026-11-12.** The publisher's page for "2017 Trust
Services Criteria (With Revised Points of Focus - 2022)" was opened:
<https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/download/2017-trust-services-criteria-with-revised-points-of-focus-2022>.
It lists one file, `Trust-services-criteria.pdf` (554.3 KB), and marks it
as locked to signed-in accounts. The page itself gives no list of
criteria and no table of contents. The agent cannot create an account.

**What is needed.** The identifier list from the maintainer's own copy.
Either of these is enough:

- the PDF, saved outside the repository, with its path given to the
  agent, which reads the criteria identifiers and hierarchy from it and
  commits identifiers and its own labels only; or
- a plain list of the criteria identifiers and which category each
  belongs to (for example `CC6.1` under common criteria, logical and
  physical access).

**What waits on it.** The SOC 2 pack, and the pilot's mappings from the
access controls to the logical access criteria. The access controls
themselves are drafted and mapped to NIST CSF PR.AA.
