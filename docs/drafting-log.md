## 2026-11-12: pilot, access controls and their NIST CSF PR.AA mappings

**What.** Nineteen new controls under `controls/access/`, 24 mappings in
`mappings/nist-csf.yaml`, and 102 tracking entries in
`tracking/nist-csf.yaml`. Two first-release controls, `human-access` and
`credential-lifecycle`, are reused in the mappings instead of being
drafted again, so no new control covers MFA on console users, MFA on
root, unused credentials or root access keys.

**How the controls were drafted.** From what a lab with one cloud account,
one code hosting organization and this application would observably do
about identities, sign-in and permissions, and only then mapped. Twelve
are manual and seven automated. None has a check: `checks` is empty on
all nineteen, and none points at an existing check that tests something
nearby.

**Counts.** PR.AA has six subcategories. Four are mapped (PR.AA-01, -02,
-03 and -05) by 24 mappings: 19 partial, 5 supporting, none direct. Two
are tracked: PR.AA-04 as not implemented and PR.AA-06 as out of scope.
106 subcategories in the pack; 4 mapped and 102 tracked.

**Placeholders.** 100 of the 102 tracking entries are placeholders. The
validator requires every requirement to be mapped or tracked, and the
pilot assesses PR.AA only. Each placeholder reads not implemented,
"Not yet assessed", with the rationale "No control has been drafted for
this subcategory." They are not worth reviewing; Wave 1 replaces them.

**Sources opened.** The NIST CSF pack above, for the PR.AA subcategory
text. No crosswalk was used: every mapping's basis is `own_analysis`.

**Least sure of.** Each carries a `reviewer_note`, except the last.

1. `access-identity-proofing`: the lab has one person, so there may be
   nothing to observe.
2. `access-single-sign-on`: the lab has no identity provider until Phase
   3, so the control reads needs attention from the start.
3. `access-offboarding-removal`, `access-role-change` and
   `access-password-length`: the deadlines and the length are the agent's
   choices, not the lab's.
4. `access-root-not-used` and `access-key-age`: each reads the report
   AWS-02 reads but asserts something different, so neither is pointed
   at AWS-02.
5. `access-workload-roles`: drafted as manual, and could be automated.
6. No note, because it is about the set and not one entry: PR.AA-05 has
   ten mappings. That may be the right picture of a broad subcategory, or
   a sign that the controls are cut too fine.
